## ✨ Features

- Early access to the Battle Frontier.
- Alternative progression through custom map connections.
- Receive the Frontier Pass and meet Scott without completing the traditional storyline.
- Access the Battle Factory without obtaining the Pokédex.
- Original Battle Factory rental Pokémon system and battle rules preserved.
- Repeatable Battle Factory challenges.
- Custom map names: Northern Path and Factory Path.
- Original Pokémon Emerald graphics and gameplay mechanics retained.

## 📦 Requirements

- An original Pokémon Emerald ROM.
- A compatible patching tool.
- The Pokémon Emerald: Battle Factory Edition patch.

**Important:** The original Pokémon Emerald ROM is not included. You must provide your own legally obtained copy.
